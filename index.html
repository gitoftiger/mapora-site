Mapora

Turn-by-turn GPS navigation for iPhone and iPad. Works online and offline across the United States.

Navigate without a signal. Download the states you need once, then get voice-guided directions, lane-aware turns and live re-routing anywhere — no data connection required.

What you get

  • Offline maps and routing for the United States, downloaded by state.
  • Door-level address search, built on U.S. National Address Database data.
  • Driving, walking and cycling routes; avoid highways and tolls; fastest or shortest route.
  • Spoken turn-by-turn directions, route preview, step-by-step directions list.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics or trackers.
