Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

In short: Mapora has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your location stays on your device. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Mapora is published by Rex Le (“we”, “us”). Contact: support@talynora.com.

Your location

Mapora uses your device’s location to show your position, calculate routes and give turn-by-turn directions, and, if you turn it on, to record your recently travelled route. Location is processed on your device. We do not receive it and we do not store it on any server.

Search and addresses

Offline search runs entirely on your device using the maps and address packs you downloaded. When you are online, the text you type into the search box, together with your approximate area, may be sent to Apple Maps (Apple MapKit) to provide suggestions. That processing is done by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. Addresses you choose or save are stored on your device so you can reuse them offline.

Downloading maps and address packs

When you download a map or address pack, your device connects to our download servers. Our servers and hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (for example IP address, date and time, and the file requested) for security and to operate the service. We do not use these logs to identify or profile you.

Purchases

If you buy Mapora Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple. We receive no payment card details; we only learn, through Apple, whether Pro is active so we can unlock it.

Data stored on your device

Bookmarks, saved addresses, settings and downloaded maps are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Contacting support

If you email us or use “Report a bug”, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, which may include device model, iOS version, app version and a diagnostic log you attach. We use it only to respond and fix problems.

Children

Mapora is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. If you live in a U.S. state with privacy rights (such as California) you may ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing support@talynora.com. Because the app keeps your data on your device, most of it can be removed by deleting the app.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
